## Methodological Implication

When all four verdicts above are positive (Exp 1.1 CONFIRMED, Exp 1.2 RECOVERED, Exp 1.3 ASYMMETRIC, XGB PHYLOGROUP_SIGNAL), the dataset-level evidence supports the *pan-genome locality mismatch* hypothesis: locality-assuming 1D CNNs are inappropriate for assembled pan-genome SNP matrices because adjacent columns no longer correspond to adjacent genomic positions. This justifies the gene-aware AMR-PhyloGene-MTL architecture proposed in Pillar 2.
